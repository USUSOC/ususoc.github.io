## An Awesome Woman In Tech

![image-center]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/image21.jpg){: .align-center}

We had over 60 students join us to hear from Winnona DeSombre Bernsen, who is a nonresident fellow with the Atlantic Council’s Cyber Statecraft Initiative under the Digital Forensic Research Lab (DFRLab) and an MPP/JD Candidate at Harvard Kennedy School and Georgetown Law. She was formerly a security engineer at Google’s Threat Analysis Group, tracking targeted threats against Google users. Bernsen has organized policy content at DEFCON in recent years and authored multiple pieces on offensive cyber capability proliferation. Winnona taught students how to start a career in cybersecurity as a successful woman in technology.
