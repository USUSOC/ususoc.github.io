## FBI Crime Lab, "Don't Be Evil"

Our club's visit to the regional digital forensics lab was an exceptional and highly valuable experience for all who attended. It was not only good but also profoundly interesting, providing us with a unique insight into the world of digital investigation. We had the opportunity to witness firsthand the advanced tools and techniques used by forensic experts to uncover digital evidence, which was both fascinating and educational. The knowledge and expertise shared by the lab's professionals left a lasting impression on all of us, emphasizing the importance of digital forensics in today's technologically driven world. While many club members found the techniques interesting, it was also a somber reminder of how a career in cybersecurity can help people who are dealing with some of the most difficult challenges in today's world. Overall, this visit was an educational, unique, and valuable experience that helped grow the connections between the club members.
